FocusGuard
Fatigue & Stress Monitor

A lightweight macOS menu-bar app that passively watches two signal streams: camera (blink rate and eye openness via MediaPipe face mesh) and keyboard (typing speed, error rate, pause length), and fuses them into live Fatigue and Stress scores. Below is the companion Flask dashboard, captured live with sample session data.
